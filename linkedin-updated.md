Why do construction teams still lose time and money even when they have all the documents?

Because evidence is everywhere, but Change Orders are managed somewhere else.

What if site logs, RFIs, plan revisions, contracts, and photos could directly feed an audit-ready Change Order workflow?

That is exactly why I built **SiteScribe**.

SiteScribe is an open-source platform that turns fragmented evidence into a single, traceable flow:  
**Evidence Upload -> Signal Detection -> Event Triage -> CO Draft -> CO Review/Approval -> Export (PDF + ZIP + SHA-256 manifest).**

The goal is simple: faster decisions, clearer traceability, and stronger contractual defensibility for project teams.

What problems does it solve, and how?

- **Problem:** Evidence is scattered across chats, emails, folders, and PDFs.  
  **Solution:** Centralized evidence workspace with structured metadata and project-level organization.
- **Problem:** Teams struggle to identify which evidence actually indicates a change risk.  
  **Solution:** Signal detection layer that scans evidence and groups findings into triage-ready events.
- **Problem:** Drafting Change Orders is slow and inconsistent between teams.  
  **Solution:** Event-based CO draft generation (template-driven + optional AI-assisted enrichment).
- **Problem:** Review cycles lack transparency and accountability.  
  **Solution:** CO editing with comments, status flow, approvals, and audit logs.
- **Problem:** Exports are often incomplete or not defensible during claims/disputes.  
  **Solution:** Export pipeline with PDF + ZIP + SHA-256 manifest for verifiable package integrity.
- **Problem:** Teams need recurring delivery and integration into external tools.  
  **Solution:** Scheduled exports, in-app notifications, and webhook support.

What features are available to users?

- Multi-tenant organizations, projects, and RBAC
- Evidence upload (PDF/image/text), extraction, chunking, and linking
- Plan revision comparison
- Signals and event triage workflow
- Change Order drafting, editing, line items, comments, and approvals
- Full-text + semantic search (optional AI mode)
- Export by download, email, and scheduled jobs
- Notifications, webhooks, friends, and 1:1 chat
- Multi-language UI support and secure-by-default architecture
